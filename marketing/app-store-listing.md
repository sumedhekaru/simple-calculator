## Description (4000 characters max)

```
Simple Best Calculator is a paper-tape calculator for everyday math: every calculation prints onto a running tape, so you can always see how you got your answer.

FEATURES
- Paper tape: every finished calculation is kept on a scrollable tape (e.g. 12.5 × 4 = 50), so you can check your work at a glance
- Add, subtract, multiply and divide, with chained operations (2 + 3 × 4)
- Percent key that works the way you expect (200 + 10% = 220)
- Delete key to fix a typo without starting over, and a ± key for negative numbers
- Thousands separators for easy reading (1,234,567.89)
- Friendly Error state for division by zero
- Calm cream-and-ink design with large, flat keys
- No ads, no tracking, no account required

Simple Best Calculator does exactly what it says: it calculates, and it shows its working. No clutter, no permissions, no data collection.
```
